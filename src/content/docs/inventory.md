### Chemicals

Several chemicals are available at the lab to be used for enlarging, they can be subdivided into several categories:

#### Ilford chemicals

Ilford brand developing chemicals, expensive, use with care. Since these must be diluted and come in bottles, be sure to use enough and then record how much is left.

| Chemical                                      | Description                                                                                          | Quantity | Dilution | Left   |
|-----------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------|----------|--------|
| <span class="tray tray-red">Developer</span>  | MULTIGRADE DEVELOPER: Liquid concentrate developer suitable for both RC and FB BW paper.             | 1        | 1:9      | 1 L    |
| <span class="tray tray-gray">Stop bath</span> | ILFOSTOP: low odour, liquid concentrate, citric acid stop bath.                                      | 1        | 1:19     | 500 ml |
| <span class="tray tray-white">Fixer</span>    | RAPID FIXED: Non-hardening, liquid concentrate fixer for traditional RC and FB photographic papers.  | 1        | 1:4      | 500 ml |

Jamarca branded chemicals, Chilean made baby C-H-I. These are single usage, so no need to measure and update the table.

| Chemical                                      | Description                  | Quantity | Dilution |
