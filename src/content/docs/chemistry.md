## Coffenol

Homemade alternative to commercially available developers using "household" materials.

### Recipes

Several recipes exist for coffenol preparation, more testing is required to establish our **own recipe**. These are the ones we have tested:
1. **Delta Recipe:** https://www.caffenol.org/recipes/
	- Water -- 1L
	- Sodium Carbonate -- 24g 
	- Ascorbic Acid -- 20g 
	- Instant Coffee -- 45g 
2. **Cahute Recipe** https://medium.com/@Cahute/our-darkroom-process-caffenol-formula-baths-baryta-drying-press-d0da27a242ad
	- Water -- 1L
	- Sodium Carbonate -- 80g 
	- Ascorbic Acid -- 4g 
	- Instant Coffee -- 40g

The currently recommended one is **number 2: Cahute Recipe**.

### Preparation

The instructions for mixing the ingredients are the same for all recipes:

1. **Dissolve sodium carbonate in hot water:** Sodium carbonate is less soluble than the rest of the ingredients so it takes hotter water and a bit longer for it to dissolve:
	- Add 200 ml of boiling water into the 500 ml graduated cylinder.
	- Add 200 ml of room temperature water into the 500 ml graduated cylinder.
	- Add the sodium carbonate.
	- Mix the solution thoroughly pouring half of the contents into the 250 ml graduated cylinder, swirling the 500 ml graduated cylinder and returning what was poured to the larger recipient _(until sodium carbonate is no longer seen in the solution)_. Do this on top of the <span class="tray tray-red">red tray</span> to catch any spillage.

3. **Add sodium carbonate solution to tray:** Pour contents of graduated cylinder into <span class="tray tray-red">red tray</span>.
4. **Dissolve ascorbic acid in warm water:** Dissolve the necessary ascorbic acid in 200 ml of room temperature water.
5. **Add sodium carbonate solution to tray:** Pour contents of graduated cylinder into <span class="tray tray-red">red tray</span>.
6. **Dissolve instant coffee in warm water:** Dissolve the instant coffee in 400 ml of room temperature water.
7. **Add instant coffee solution to tray:** Pour contents of graduated cylinder into <span class="tray tray-red">red tray</span>.
8. **Mix contents of tray:** Move the <span class="tray tray-red">red tray</span> to ensure everything is thoroughly mixed.

## Ilford

The instructions for Ilford chemicals are pretty straightforward: dilute the chemicals in 20-25 degrees Celsius water and pour them on their corresponding trays. All instructions are for 1 liter of solution.

1. <span class="tray tray-red">Developer:</span> Dilute 100 ml of Ilford Multigrade Developer in 900 ml of water. Pour it into the <span class="tray tray-red">red tray</span>.
2. <span class="tray tray-gray">Stop Bath:</span> Dilute 50 ml of Ilford Multigrade Developer in 950 ml of water. Pour it into the <span class="tray tray-gray">gray tray</span>.
3. <span class="tray tray-white">Fixer:</span> Dilute 200 ml of Ilford Rapid Fixer in 800 ml of water. Pour it into the <span class="tray tray-white">white tray</span>.

## Jamarca

The instructions for Jamarca chemicals are pretty straightforward: dissolve the chemicals in 20-30 degrees Celsius water and pour them on their corresponding trays. All instructions are for 1 liter of solution.

1. <span class="tray tray-red">Developer:</span> _untested_.
2. <span class="tray tray-gray">Stop Bath:</span> _untested_.
3. <span class="tray tray-white">Fixer:</span> Dissolve contents of Jamarca Fijador Universal in 1000 ml of 30-35 degrees celsius water. Pour it into the <span class="tray tray-white">white tray</span>.
